# Code of conduct

This covers the places the project lives: issues, discussions and anything else
under the `gettori` organization.

## What is expected

- Be kind and assume the other person means well. Most sharp-sounding messages
  are written in a hurry or in a second language.
- Argue about the thing, never the person. "This breaks on a worktree with no
  upstream" is useful. "Did you even test this" is not.
- Say what you saw and what you expected. Reports with steps get fixed;
  complaints without them mostly do not.
- Take a no gracefully. One person maintains Tori and decides what goes in. A
  declined request is a decision about the project, not about you.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or remarks about someone's background, identity, body or
  ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Posting someone else's private information.
- Repeatedly reopening a settled question to wear the other side down.
- Spam, including machine-written issues nobody read before posting.

## What happens

The maintainer removes content that crosses the line and says why. Someone who
keeps at it after being asked to stop is blocked from the organization. For
something serious, that happens without a warning.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the menu on the comment or issue. The report goes to
the maintainer and is not shown to anyone else. If the problem is the
maintainer, report it to GitHub the same way and choose to report to GitHub
rather than to the repository.
